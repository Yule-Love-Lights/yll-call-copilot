# The Weekly Role-Play Routine

> Written 2026-08-21. A repeatable weekly ritual that turns your real calls into practice, so the team
> gets better on purpose instead of by accident. Built on Hormozi's training method (one skill at a
> time, fast feedback, drill it) and your own scorecard. Pairs with
> `TRAINING-pain-cycle-discovery.md` and `ROLEPLAY-01-quote-followup-no-discovery.md`.

## The idea in one line
Every week, pull the worst real call, turn it into a role-play, and drill one skill until it's smooth.
Small and consistent beats a big training event once a year.

## The weekly loop (five steps)

### 1. Friday or over the weekend: pick the call and build the scenario
Pull last week's weakest real conversation (lowest discovery or emotional-diagnosis score, and a real
conversation, not a voicemail). Turn it into a role-play in the same shape as Role-Play #1: what went
wrong, the re-do, and a persona card. This takes a few minutes.
- Who does it: Naldo, in a quick Claude/Cowork session, or the copilot surfaces the worst call for you.
- The repeatable request: "Pull the lowest-discovery real conversation from last week, anonymize it,
  and build a role-play like ROLEPLAY-01 with a coaching key, a re-do, and a persona card."

### 2. Monday huddle, 15 minutes: run it
- Coach names ONE skill focus for the week (see the rotation below). One, not five.
- Coach plays the homeowner from the persona card. A rep plays the rep.
- Fast feedback in real time: "good, keep going," or "pause, try that line again." Not a list at the end.
- When the rep says the target line right, have them say it right five times in a row so it sticks.
- Player-coach goes first and shares their own misses, so nobody's afraid to look bad.

### 3. During the week: reps practice on their own
Between calls, two minutes each: run the scenario against a teammate or against Claude ("play a guarded
homeowner and score me on whether I found and named your pain"). Reps only. Just the one skill.

### 4. Midweek: celebrate a real win
Find a real call from this week where a rep actually nailed the week's skill and play that good tape for
the team. Wins get shared out loud. Rough calls get coached one on one, never on the board.

### 5. Next Monday: new call, next skill
Fresh worst-call scenario, next skill in the rotation. The loop repeats.

## The 8-week skill rotation
Drill one per week, then start over. Repetition is the point.
1. Discovery: ask 3 real questions before any price.
2. Overview the pain: ask what they've done before and quantify the cost.
3. Name it back: label the busy/excited/guarded/status type out loud.
4. Value stack: say what's included and both guarantee lines before the number.
5. Price with confidence: state it once, clean, then pause.
6. Objection handling: acknowledge, associate, ask (the AAA method).
7. The close: binary choice, confirm decision-makers, recap.
8. The warm ending and the referral ask.

## The rules that keep it safe
- One correction at a time. People can't fix five things at once.
- Scores and wins are shared with the team. Corrections are always one on one.
- Improvement is celebrated as loudly as the top score. Nobody's pinned to the bottom.
- The player-coach's own calls are on the board too. This is training, not a ranking of who to fire.

## How it ties to the copilot
The scenario is scored on the same rubric your copilot uses on real calls, so practice and the real
thing are graded the same way. Over time, the weekly worst-call becomes the weekly training focus, and
the whole team drifts upward on the exact dimension that was weakest.

## Optional: make it automatic later
If you want, this can become a scheduled task that pulls the worst call and drafts the week's scenario
every Friday, so it's waiting for you Monday. Say the word and we'll wire it up. Until then, running it
by hand takes ten minutes a week and works exactly the same.
