# SCRIPT 2: THE REBOOK CALL (past customers, August and September)
**Why it matters:** returning customers are the cheapest money you have. The industry rebooks 60% to
70% when asked early. The whole job is to make saying yes almost effortless.

### Opener
**You:** "Happy Holidays [name], it's [rep] with Yule Love Lights. [Off-season option: 'Hi [name], it's
[rep] with Yule Love Lights, hope your summer's been great.'] I'm reaching out early because I'd love to
get you back on the calendar before the season fills up. Got a quick minute?"

### Reconnect and find the gap (short pain cycle)
- "First, how did last season go for you? Anything you loved, anything you'd tweak this year?"
- *(If they loved it: bank it. If they'd change something: great, you get to fix it and it's a reason to rebook.)*
- "Remember the November scramble everyone goes through? The whole point of calling now is you skip all that. Your spot's held and this year's price is locked."

### The offer (make it one-tap easy)
**You:** "Here's what I can do: I'll hold your install spot and last year's pricing for you, same
beautiful design or we tweak it, whatever you want. All you have to do is say the word and you're on the
schedule, done for the season. Want me to lock it in?"
- All-inclusive and the 48-hour fix still apply, say them if they hesitate.

### Objections
- **"Money's tight this year."** "Totally fair. Locking in now actually protects you from the price going
  up, and it's the same all-in number, no surprise takedown or storage fees. Want me to hold your spot
  while you decide, so at least you don't lose it?"
- **"We might not do it this year."** "No pressure at all. Can I hold your spot and price for two weeks
  so it's there if you want it? Costs you nothing." *(Takeaway, low friction.)*
- **"We moved / different house."** "Congrats! Want me to design something for the new place? I'd love
  to light it up for you."

### Close and referral
**You:** "Perfect, you're locked in for [window]. And since you've seen how it works, a lot of our repeat
customers refer a neighbor, you get a $100 credit and we light up their place too. Anyone on your street
come to mind?"

# SCRIPT 4: THE REFERRAL AND SECOND-MILE CALL (right after the reveal)
**Why it matters:** a thrilled customer is the cheapest lead source there is. Timing is everything,
make this call right after install, when they've just seen their house lit up.

### Opener
**You:** "Happy Holidays [name], it's [rep] with Yule Love Lights. [Off-season/permanent option: 'Hi
[name]...'] Our crew just sent me the photo of your house, it came out beautiful. Did you get to see it
lit up last night?"
- *(Let them gush. This call is about their feeling first.)*

### The genuine check-in (second mile, not a pitch yet)
- "I just wanted to make sure you're thrilled with it. Anything at all you want adjusted?"
- *(Reference a personal detail if you have one: the party, the kids, first Christmas in the new house.)*

### The referral ask (make it about helping their friend)
**You:** "Can I ask you a quick favor? The best compliment we get is when a happy customer sends us a
neighbor. If you know someone who'd love their house done like yours, we'll give you a $100 credit, and
we'll light up their place with two free spritzers as a gift from you. Anyone come to mind, maybe someone
on your street who always says how good yours looks?"
- *(Ask for a specific name. "Anyone come to mind" beats "keep us in mind" every time.)*

### If they hesitate (Acknowledge, Associate, Ask)
**You:** "Totally understand, no pressure. A lot of folks think of someone later, so I'll text you the
referral link and you can just pass it along whenever it's easy. Sound good?"

### Close
**You:** "You made our week. Enjoy the season, and we'll see you for takedown in January, we handle all
of it. Happy Holidays, [name]."

# SCRIPT 6: MISSED-CALL TEXT-BACK (automated, instant)
**Why it matters:** when the phone rings and you can't grab it, that lead is one tap away from calling a
competitor. An instant text keeps them yours. This is speed-to-lead for the calls you miss.

### Text 1 (fires the moment a call is missed, within seconds)
"Hi, this is Yule Love Lights, sorry we just missed your call! This is [rep], a real person. Were you
reaching out about holiday lights for your home? Happy to help right here or hop on a quick call,
whichever's easier for you."

### Text 2 (if no reply in ~15 minutes)
"No worries if now's not a good time. Quick question so I can help fast: are you thinking the whole
house, or mainly the roofline? Happy Holidays!"

### Text 3 (next day, if still no reply)
"Hi [name], still happy to get you a design of your home so you can see what it'd look like, no cost, no
commitment. Want me to put one together?"

### Rules
- Always sound like a real person, never an obvious auto-bot.
- End every text on an easy question or a clear yes/no. That's what gets a reply.
- If they reply, a person takes over immediately and runs the inbound script.

# SCRIPT 7: THE INBOUND PRICE-SHOPPER QUALIFIER
**Why it matters:** the caller who opens with "just tell me how much" is not being difficult, they just
don't know how to compare yet. Don't blurt a number (you'd be guessing), and don't dodge. Slow it down,
get two facts, and turn the price question into a conversation.

### The open (Happy Holidays, then don't answer blind)
**Caller:** "Hi, how much for holiday lights?"
**You:** "Happy Holidays, great question, and I'll get you a real number, not a made-up one. It depends
on the house, so if I just threw out a price I'd be guessing, and I don't want to do that to you. Two
quick things and I can get you close. Are you thinking mainly the front roofline, or the whole house,
trees and walkway too?"

### Get the second fact + a sliver of the why
- "Got it. And is this to make it look amazing for the family, or more about never dealing with the
  ladder yourself?" *(One quick discovery question, framed as helping you price it.)*

### Give a real range, wrapped in value (then pivot)
**You:** "Okay, for a home like yours, most folks land somewhere in the [$X to $Y] range for the season,
and here's what that includes: the design, the install, all-season service, the takedown in January, and
storage, all in one number, no hidden fees. Plus if a section ever goes dark, we fix it in 48 hours or
your month is free. The best next step is a quick design of your actual home so you see it and get an
exact number. I've got [day] or [day], which works?"

### If they only want the cheapest number and won't engage
**You:** "Totally fair if price is the main thing. I'll be honest, we're not the cheapest, because the
cheap quotes usually leave out takedown, storage, and service, and they won't fix a dark section in
writing. If you want the house handled start to finish with none of that hassle, we're worth a look.
Want me to send a free design so you've got a real comparison?"
- *(Never drop the price to win a shopper. Offer the design, let the value do the work, and let the
  wrong-fit ones go.)*

## Non-negotiables (all four scripts)
- [ ] Opened warm and branded.
- [ ] Found at least one thing about them before pitching (even a fast one).
- [ ] Said the value and the guarantee lines before or with any number.
- [ ] Made the next step easy and specific (a locked spot, a booked time, a link).
- [ ] On a happy customer, asked for the referral with a specific name.
